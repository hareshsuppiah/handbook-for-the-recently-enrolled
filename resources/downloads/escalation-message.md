Escalation message builder

Privacy screen

- [ ] I am contacting the correct authorised recipient.
- [ ] I have included only information needed for this decision.
- [ ] Sensitive evidence is stored in an approved location, not broadly attached.
- [ ] I have avoided speculation about motives or diagnoses.
- [ ] I have named any immediate safety, participant, privacy or integrity concern clearly.

Blank message

Subject: Decision/support needed by [date]: [specific issue]

Hello [name/role],

I am requesting [the exact decision, protection, review or action].

The current position is [two or three factual sentences: expected state, observed state and relevant dates]. The immediate impact is [milestone, work, participant, safety or other consequence].

I have already [actions and results]. The relevant record is [authorised location or concise reference].

The feasible options I can see are:

1. [option and main consequence];
2. [option and main consequence]; and
3. [option and main consequence].

My provisional recommendation is [option] because [bounded reason]. I need [decision/action] by [date] because [trigger or consequence]. Please let me know if this should instead use [known formal route].

Regards,

[name]

Before sending: replace bracketed prompts; remove unused options; use the authorised urgent or independent route where required.
