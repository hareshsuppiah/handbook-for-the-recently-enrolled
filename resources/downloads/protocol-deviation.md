Protocol-deviation log

Follow the ethics, governance, safety, clinical, regulatory, site, cultural and institutional process that applies. Use this log alongside required notification or urgent action.

Immediate response

If participant or community welfare, animal welfare, safety, confidentiality, data integrity or legal authority may be affected, stop the affected activity where safe, protect people and records, and contact the responsible local authority.
