Response pattern

For each point:

1. thank or acknowledge without empty repetition;
2. state the concern you understood;
3. explain what you did and why;
4. identify the exact changed location or explain a reasoned non-change; and
5. quote only the short passage needed to make verification easy.

Conflict and new-analysis checks

- [ ] Editor instructions take precedence where they define the decision, but scientific or ethical concerns are raised explicitly.
- [ ] Conflicting reviewer requests are shown rather than silently choosing one.
- [ ] New analyses have a stated purpose and are labelled planned, amended or exploratory accurately.
- [ ] Any protocol, registration, ethics, data, code or authorship implications are addressed.
- [ ] Abstract, tables, figures, supplement and response letter agree.
- [ ] Every author approves the revised manuscript and response.
