# Supervision agreement

From The Handbook for the Recently Enrolled, edited by Haresh Suppiah. CC BY 4.0.

Supervision agreement

Complete any mandatory institutional form separately. Confirm required review schedules, meeting frequency, workload allocation and authority for supervisory-team changes. Example timings do not establish this team's agreement.

Blank agreement

Purpose and scope:
[What this agreement covers and which formal policy sits above it.]

Supervisory team and roles:
[Name roles, not private personal information in a public copy.]

Decision authority:
[Student provisional decisions; whole-team decisions; formal or specialist decisions.]

Meeting cadence and preparation:
[Frequency, agenda timing, papers and cancellation arrangements.]

Communication and urgency:
[Routine channel, urgent route, reasonable boundaries.]

Feedback:
[Reviewable material, requested focus, ordinary turnaround, long-work arrangements.]

Records:
[Who records decisions and actions, where they are stored, correction window.]

Continuity and absence:
[How planned and unexpected absence will be handled.]

Development and independence:
[Priority skills, networks, decision responsibilities and review of progress.]

Authorship and contributions:
[When and how contribution expectations will be recorded and revisited.]

Concerns and escalation:
[Current independent support and formal routes; do not include sensitive case details.]

Review date:
[Date and trigger events.]

Acknowledgement:
[How participants confirm that the record reflects the discussion.]
