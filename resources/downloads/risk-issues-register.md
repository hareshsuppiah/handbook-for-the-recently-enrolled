Risk and issues register

Classification key

- Risk: an uncertain future event or condition.
- Issue: the event has happened or the condition exists.
- Waiting: progress depends on a known external action.
- Blocked: the current route cannot produce the required outcome.
- Incident: harm, breach, loss or unsafe conduct may have occurred; use the authorised urgent process.
