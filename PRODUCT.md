# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Quarto book authored in QMD, rendered primarily to accessible HTML, versioned with Git, and published through GitHub Pages.

## Users

The primary users are PhD students, especially people who are newly enrolled or facing an unfamiliar decision during candidature. Secondary users include research master's students, supervisors, graduate research coordinators, librarians, methodologists, data stewards, research software staff, integrity and ethics advisers, and research support staff.

## Product Purpose

Help graduate researchers decide what to do next, conduct due diligence, use supervision well, maintain defensible research records, recognise avoidable failure, recover from disruption, and grow towards independent judgement.

Success means a reader can find their question quickly, understand the judgement involved, take a small useful next action, use a practical resource, and recognise when local advice or formal escalation is required.

## Positioning

This is a question-led research operating manual rather than a policy handbook or generic collection of study tips. Chapters connect explanation, decisions, worked examples, short checklists, reusable templates, supervisor preparation, and stuck triage.

## Operating Context

Readers use the book throughout candidature: before meetings, while choosing topics and methods, during evidence searching and analysis, when writing, when plans fail, and during examination and closure. The project itself is maintained publicly through source registers, question and coverage records, version control, documented editorial decisions, and community suggestions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Institution-neutral core with explicit method, discipline, institution, and jurisdiction variation.
- Thirty-six navigation chapters covering all 82 source topics.
- Fifty-three tracked checklists and templates.
- Full-book search, collapsible Parts, per-page contents, and a direct reader-feedback route.
- Verified references; no fabricated citations or universalised local policy.
- Public repository from first pushed commit, while the raw source conversation remains private until approved.
- CC BY 4.0 content and MIT code/configuration.
- Australian/British spelling.

## Brand Commitments

Exact title: **The Handbook for the Recently Enrolled**.  
Subtitle: **An Operating Manual for Graduate Researchers**.  
Founding author and editor: **Haresh Suppiah**.

The cover is an original uncanny academic field manual: distressed dark-brown surfaces, cream block title, dusty-pink italic `Recently Enrolled`, and a lower painterly research vignette. The reading interface is deliberately separate: modern black text, a white background, restrained grey rules and sans-serif typography modelled on the structural clarity of *R for the Rest of Us*. The cover may acknowledge the general idea of a mysterious manual but must not copy *Beetlejuice* artwork, typography, logos, composition or branded details.

## Product Principles

1. Questions before topics.
2. Decision support before encyclopaedic coverage.
3. Due diligence before dependency.
4. Checklists as short safety infrastructure.
5. Stuck triage as a first-class navigation system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The book must support keyboard use, responsive layouts, clear headings, high contrast, descriptive alternative text, readable print output, plain-language contribution routes, and variation across study mode, experience, discipline, method, institution, and jurisdiction.
