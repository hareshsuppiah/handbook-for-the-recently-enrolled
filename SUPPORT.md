# Support boundaries

This repository accepts questions and reports about the handbook itself: unclear guidance, missing topics, evidence suggestions, broken links, accessibility problems, or technical defects.

Do not use public issues for:

- confidential supervision disputes;
- participant or research data;
- personal welfare, medical, counselling, or emergency support;
- formal complaints, grievances, misconduct allegations, or research-integrity casework;
- ethics or governance approval for a live study;
- legal advice;
- passwords, credentials, private correspondence, or unpublished sensitive findings.

Use the relevant local supervisor, graduate research office, ethics office, research integrity adviser, complaints process, wellbeing service, emergency service, or other formal channel. This handbook cannot determine which local process applies to an individual case.
