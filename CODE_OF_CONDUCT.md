# Code of conduct

This project welcomes constructive contributions from students, supervisors, researchers, and research-support professionals.

Be respectful, specific, evidence-aware, and attentive to differences in discipline, method, institution, jurisdiction, culture, and lived experience. Do not harass, demean, expose private information, or turn a personal experience into a universal claim.

Public project spaces must not contain participant data, confidential supervision disputes, unpublished sensitive material, credentials, or identifying information about third parties.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that creates a privacy, safety, legal, integrity, or conduct risk. Concerns about project conduct may be raised privately with the repository owner; confidential academic, welfare, integrity, ethics, or emergency matters must use appropriate local channels instead.
