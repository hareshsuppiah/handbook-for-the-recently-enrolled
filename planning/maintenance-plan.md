# Maintenance plan

## Intake

Specific public requests enter through structured issues. Broad ideas may begin in Discussions. No-GitHub submissions are privacy-checked before being represented in the public tracker or community register.

## Triage

1. Preserve the exact question or problem.
2. Remove unnecessary personal or sensitive detail.
3. Check duplicates and current coverage.
4. Classify phase, type, scope, risk, variation, and evidence need.
5. Link or add the question in the question bank.
6. Run the targeted coverage loop for the affected and neighbouring phases.
7. Verify evidence and local-versus-universal scope.
8. Update all affected chapters, resources, examples, cross-links, and stuck routes.
9. Render and test.
10. Record the decision, release, and contributor response.

## Labels

- Type: content, correction, evidence, checklist-template, accessibility, technical, local-adaptation.
- Status: needs-triage, accepted, needs-evidence, in-progress, review, released, declined, duplicate.
- Phase: pre-enrolment, first-90-days, topic, literature, design, ethics, data, analysis, writing, publication, examination, transition, disruption.
- Risk: routine, consequential, high-risk.
- Scope: universal-core, method-specific, discipline-specific, institution-local, time-sensitive.

## Review cycle

- Targeted review on every substantive request or relevant change.
- Full coverage audit before every major release.
- Time-sensitive pages use `last-reviewed`, `review-by`, and `review-owner` metadata.
- A bounded audit may stop after two consecutive passes find no substantive additions, but the handbook is never described as permanently complete.
