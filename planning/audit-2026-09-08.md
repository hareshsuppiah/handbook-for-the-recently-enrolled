Baseline: commit `8a350d3`. The owner's 5/10 is the starting assessment, not a measured usability score. This review covers every published source page, all 36 chapters, practical resources, contribution guidance, image placements and the rendered reading experience. Page-level records accompany this report. A 10/10 cannot be certified by the authoring agent: first-time reader testing remains a separate evidence gate.

## Browser findings and corrections

Live Chrome checks found and corrected wide-template table overflow at 320px; tables now sit in a keyboard-focusable scrolling region and blank template text wraps. Checklist controls were moved after the page's orientation, and phone pages gain an expanded local contents list. Print has a static full-width layout and opens practice/template disclosures for printing, restoring their state afterwards. These changes address specific observed or independently reproduced defects, rather than treating successful rendering as sufficient.

Independent tool review found that a file-URL feedback link could include a local filesystem path, copied checklist ticks omitted decision trees and stop conditions, and downloadable blanks were not verified against current source. The feedback route now derives a bounded relative public path; Copy checklist retains the full guidance and warnings; download generation fails without exactly one blank and the verifier checks exact content. Reset provides undo and restores keyboard focus. The resource finder now filters the complete generated catalogue, rather than only a six-row quick-start table.
